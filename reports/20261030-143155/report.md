## Confirmed findings

### QA-001 - Penang destination results repeatedly return HTTP 503

**Severity:** Medium. Penang browsing is unavailable, while other destinations remain usable.  
**Reproduction:** Select Penang from Discover. The same destination selection returned HTTP 503 three times. Kuala Lumpur, Langkawi, and Cameron Highlands provided working comparisons.  
**Actual:** Results unavailable alert, HTTP 503, and no Penang cards. The visible collection includes The George in Penang, so the destination option is not merely an unlisted location.  
**Root cause:** Not established.  
**Evidence:** [Penang error screenshot](evidence/screenshots/EVID-007-penang-error-visible.png), [network observations](evidence/network/network-observations.md), [console observations](evidence/console/console-observations.md).

### QA-002 - The Moss House quote accepts more guests than its stated room limit

**Severity:** Medium. A party can receive a quote above the property's explicit capacity, although selecting four or fewer is a practical workaround.  
**Reproduction:** Open The Moss House, select six guests, and inspect the quote. The detail says “Up to 4 guests” and “A maximum of 4 guests.” The selector offers 5 and 6; `/api/quote` returned 200, displayed RM 3,726.00, and left Continue to reservation available. Reproduced twice.  
**Root cause:** Not established. The later reservation step was not exercised.  
**Evidence:** [Full-page capacity screenshot](evidence/screenshots/EVID-008-moss-capacity-fullpage.png), [network observations](evidence/network/network-observations.md).
